Month 2, four weeks, then the checkpoint. About 40 learning hours, lab time included. Everything serves the anchor answer.

Assumes month 1 is done: dev machine running Ubuntu 26.04 and ROS 2 Lyrical, ROS 2 fundamentals (nodes, topics, launch files, bags), and the thin maritime pass.

**Setup rule:** sort friction before timeboxing it. Camera and GNSS driver bring-up, device permissions, topic, frame and timestamp mismatches are integration practice and count as learning time; log each one as a failure journal entry. Build-system noise, dependency conflicts and flashing problems are not: timebox those at 90 minutes, post to the forum, switch to a lab that runs on existing bags, and come back.

**If you fail the checkpoint,** do not start the next month's material. Spend its first week rerunning week 3 and the blind drill; state estimation is the base the rest of the plan stands on.
